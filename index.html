Integrated Transition Assessment — company scorecard

How is this scored?

The basics

Every indicator is assessed as met (worth 1), partly met (worth ½) or not met (worth 0).

Within each dimension, the indicators are grouped into three measurement areas. A measurement-area score is the share of its indicators that were met, from 0 to 100.

A dimension score combines the three measurement areas: planning counts for 40%, implementation for 40% and performance for 20%, rounded to a whole number.

The ITA score is the plain average of the three dimension scores, shown to one decimal. Companies are ranked on their ITA score and on each dimension score, both among all 2,000 companies and within their sector.

The six integration checks are reported separately and are not counted in the ITA score.

Worked example

Suppose a company's climate results are:

  • Planning (18 indicators): 11 met, 4 partly met, 3 not met → (11 + 4×½) ⁄ 18 = 13 ⁄ 18 → measurement-area score 72
  • Implementation (9 indicators): 4 met, 2 partly met, 3 not met → (4 + 2×½) ⁄ 9 = 5 ⁄ 9 → measurement-area score 56
  • Performance (1 indicator): partly met → ½ ⁄ 1 → measurement-area score 50

Climate dimension score = 0.4×72 + 0.4×56 + 0.2×50 = 28.8 + 22.4 + 10 = 61.

If the nature dimension score is 48 and the social dimension score is 65, the ITA score = (61 + 48 + 65) ⁄ 3 = 58.0.
